## What this company is

MBrace Therapeutics was co-founded by Renata Pasqualini, Ph.D. and Isan Chen, MD. Its lead candidate
MBRC-101 is an anti-EphA5 monomethyl auristatin E (MMAE) antibody-drug conjugate in a multi-centre,
open-label Phase 1/1b dose-finding, safety and pharmacokinetic study in advanced refractory solid
tumours. MBRC-201 is in Phase 2, MBRC-301 is in IND-enabling studies against solid tumours, and
MBRC-401 is in discovery. The company emerged from stealth in November 2023 with an $85 million
Series B led by TPG through TPG Life Sciences Innovations, alongside Avidity Partners, Blue Owl
Capital, Venrock and Alta Partners, for $110 million raised in total. MBRC-101 was named Most
Promising Clinical Candidate at the 11th Annual World ADC Awards.
